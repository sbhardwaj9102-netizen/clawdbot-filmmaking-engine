# NEON REQUIEM — Cold Open

EXT. HARBOR DISTRICT ROOFTOP — NIGHT

Rain. A city of flickering signage below.

DETECTIVE MARA VOSS (45) stands at the roof's edge, staring at a single red umbrella abandoned in the downpour.

She crouches. Beneath the umbrella: a brass pocket watch, stopped at 3:17.

MARA
(to herself)
Same time as the last one.

A shadow moves on the water tower behind her. She turns — nothing but steam venting from a rooftop duct.

She pockets the watch and walks toward the stairwell door. It's already open.
