# NEON REQUIEM — Production Blueprint (Cold Open)

## Phase 1: Style Profile

- **Aspect Ratio:** 2.39:1 anamorphic. Generate in Firefly at 16:9 widescreen and crop to 2.39:1 in post. Keep critical action inside the center 75% of frame height so the crop never clips a face.
- **Camera DNA:** Arri Alexa LF look with Cooke Anamorphic/i FF lenses (40mm for wides, 75mm for singles, 100mm macro inserts). Oval bokeh, horizontal cyan lens flares from practicals, shallow depth of field at T2.3.
- **Color Grade:** Desaturated teal-and-sodium split. Shadows crushed to blue-black, highlights skewed toward sodium orange and magenta neon. The red umbrella is the **only** saturated red in the sequence; nothing else in frame may carry red.
- **Lighting Blueprint:** Low-key neo-noir. Hard magenta and cyan neon spill from below the roofline acts as underlight. Cold overcast skyglow is the key. Backlit rain streaks against the practicals, and volumetric steam catches the light in shafts.

## Phase 2: Shot Sequence

### 🎬 Shot 1: Extreme Wide / High-Angle Establishing
- **Narrative Beat:** The camera finds a lone figure on a rain-lashed rooftop above a sprawling neon harbor district.
- **Composition & Framing:** High-angle crane-style establishing shot. The subject sits small on the lower-third right; the city fills the frame's upper two-thirds.
- **Adobe Firefly Image 3 Prompt:**
```text
A small lone figure of a 45-year-old woman detective in a heavily weathered, rain-slicked charcoal grey trench coat with a turned-up high collar stands at the edge of a wet tar-paper rooftop, seen from high above. Below her, a dense harbor district of stacked tenements and cargo cranes glows with flickering magenta and cyan neon signage reflected in flooded streets. Heavy diagonal rain streaks, low overcast clouds lit orange from beneath by sodium city glow, steam rising from rooftop vents. Shot on Arri Alexa LF with a 40mm Cooke anamorphic lens, deep teal shadows, horizontal cyan lens flare, fine 35mm film grain, wide cinematic frame.
```
- **Structure Reference Guide:** Upload a simple grayscale perspective sketch. Draw a rooftop ledge as a hard diagonal line from lower-left to mid-right, with a small vertical figure silhouette (about 8% of frame height) standing on the right third of that ledge. Fill the upper 65% of the canvas with rectangular building blocks falling away in two-point perspective. Set Structure strength to about 60 so Firefly keeps the high-angle geometry but still invents the city detail.

### 🎬 Shot 2: Medium Close-Up / Over-the-Shoulder Reveal
- **Narrative Beat:** Mara stares down at a single red umbrella lying open and abandoned in the downpour.
- **Composition & Framing:** Over-the-shoulder from behind Mara's left shoulder. Her collar and wet hair take up the left third out of focus, and the umbrella sits sharp in the right-center middle ground.
- **Adobe Firefly Image 3 Prompt:**
```text
Over-the-shoulder view from behind a 45-year-old woman detective with a wet, dark shoulder-length bob, wearing a heavily weathered, rain-slicked charcoal grey trench coat with a turned-up high collar, out of focus in the left foreground. In sharp focus beyond her, a single vivid crimson umbrella lies open and upturned on a puddled tar-paper rooftop, rainwater pooling in its canopy. Magenta neon underlight bouncing off wet surfaces, backlit rain streaks, cold blue skyglow. Arri Alexa LF, 75mm Cooke anamorphic lens at T2.3, oval bokeh, desaturated teal palette with the umbrella as the only saturated red, fine film grain.
```
- **Structure Reference Guide:** Upload a two-plane depth sketch. Put a large dark soft-edged mass (the shoulder and collar) covering the left 35% of the frame from the bottom edge up to about 70% height. On the right-center, draw a clean dome or semicircle outline for the upturned umbrella, sitting on a horizontal ground line at 60% frame height. Set Structure strength to about 70 to lock the foreground/background separation.

### 🎬 Shot 3: Macro Insert / Top-Down
- **Narrative Beat:** Under the umbrella, a brass pocket watch lies stopped at 3:17.
- **Composition & Framing:** Top-down macro insert. The watch face is dead center with the hands clearly readable, and the red umbrella canopy edge bleeds into the top of frame.
- **Adobe Firefly Image 3 Prompt:**
```text
Top-down macro close-up of an antique brass pocket watch with a scratched, fogged crystal and cracked white enamel face, its black hands stopped at 3:17, resting on wet black tar paper beaded with raindrops. The soft red edge of an umbrella canopy curves across the top of the frame, casting a faint crimson glow onto the brass. Single raindrops frozen mid-splash, magenta neon reflected in the watch crystal. 100mm macro lens, razor-thin depth of field, tarnished metal texture, teal shadows, fine 35mm film grain.
```
- **Structure Reference Guide:** Upload a centered circle sketch at about 45% of frame width, with two hand lines drawn at the 3:17 position (hour hand just past 3, minute hand on the 17-minute mark) and a small crown nub at 12 o'clock. Add a soft arc across the top 15% of the frame for the umbrella edge. Set Structure strength to 85 or higher. Firefly drifts on clock-hand positions, so if the hands still come out wrong, inpaint them with Generative Fill.

### 🎬 Shot 4: Medium Shot / Low-Angle Whip-Turn
- **Narrative Beat:** Mara spins toward the water tower, where a shadow vanishes into venting steam.
- **Composition & Framing:** Low-angle medium shot from knee height. Mara is caught mid-turn in the left third, and the water tower silhouette towers upper-right, wrapped in steam.
- **Adobe Firefly Image 3 Prompt:**
```text
Low-angle medium shot of a 45-year-old woman detective with a wet, dark shoulder-length bob, wearing a heavily weathered, rain-slicked charcoal grey trench coat with a turned-up high collar, caught mid-turn with her coat hem flaring and her hand reaching into her coat, her face tense and lit by hard cyan neon from below. Behind her, the black silhouette of a riveted wooden rooftop water tower rises into the rain, half-swallowed by thick white steam venting from a rusted duct, a faint humanoid shadow dissolving into the vapor. Volumetric light shafts through the steam, motion-blurred rain. Arri Alexa LF, 40mm Cooke anamorphic lens, crushed blue-black shadows, sodium orange skyglow, fine film grain.
```
- **Structure Reference Guide:** Upload a low-angle figure pose sketch. Use a stick figure or mannequin in the left third with its torso twisted about 45° toward the right, one arm bent into the coat, and the lower frame edge cutting at the knees. In the upper-right, draw a tall cylindrical tank on four stilt legs, with its top edge near the frame's top. Scribble a loose cloud shape across the tank's base. Set Structure strength to about 55 so the steam stays organic.

### 🎬 Shot 5: Wide / Rear Tracking Shot
- **Narrative Beat:** Mara pockets the watch and walks toward the stairwell door, which is already standing open.
- **Composition & Framing:** Rear tracking wide shot from behind at waist height. Mara sits center frame, walking away toward a lit door in the vanishing point, so the open doorway reads as the focal threat.
- **Adobe Firefly Image 3 Prompt:**
```text
View from behind of a 45-year-old woman detective with a wet, dark shoulder-length bob, wearing a heavily weathered, rain-slicked charcoal grey trench coat with a turned-up high collar, walking away down the center of a flooded rooftop toward a rusted steel stairwell door standing ajar. A thin blade of harsh yellow-white fluorescent light spills from the gap across the wet ground. Rain curtains backlit by the doorway, puddles reflecting magenta neon, steam drifting across the path. Arri Alexa LF, 40mm Cooke anamorphic lens, symmetrical one-point perspective, deep teal shadows, horizontal lens flare from the door light, fine 35mm film grain.
```
- **Structure Reference Guide:** Upload a one-point perspective sketch. Draw converging floor lines meeting at a vanishing point in the upper-center, with a tall rectangle (the door) at that point and a thin bright wedge opening on its right edge. Put a vertical figure silhouette dead center at about 40% of frame height, cut off at the knees by the bottom edge. Set Structure strength to about 65.

**Continuity rules for the VFX desk**
1. **Red discipline:** `PROP_UMBRELLA` owns the only red in the sequence. If Firefly paints red neon anywhere else, repaint it magenta with Generative Fill.
2. **Watch time:** 3:17 is a plot point. Check the hands on every frame where the watch appears.
3. **Style Reference lock:** once Shot 1 is approved, load it as the Firefly **Style Reference** (strength about 40) for Shots 2–5 so grain, grade and flare stay consistent.
4. **Screen direction:** Mara faces screen-right in Shots 1–2, turns screen-left in Shot 4, and exits away from camera in Shot 5. Flip any generation that breaks this.
